Service overview

Accessory dwelling unit construction, done properly

An accessory dwelling unit is a full home in a compact footprint. It needs its own foundation, framing, systems and finishes, and every square foot has to work hard.

We build ADUs as a general contractor, coordinating with your designer and engineer and carrying the project from foundation through turnkey finishes.

Scope

What this service covers

The exact scope follows your approved plans and agreement. These are the parts we typically coordinate.

01

Site preparation

Preparing the area for the ADU foundation according to the plans.

02

Foundation and framing

Structure built to the approved engineering.

03

Utility connections

Plumbing, electrical and mechanical systems for a complete living unit.

04

Kitchen and bathroom

Compact kitchens and bathrooms planned with the layout and fixtures.

05

Exterior finishes

Roofing, windows, doors and siding or stucco.

06

Interior finishes

Floors, cabinetry, tile, lighting and paint through completion.
