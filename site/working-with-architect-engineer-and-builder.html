How your architect, engineer and builder work together

New homes, additions and ADUs start with plans. Here is how the design team and your general contractor fit together, step by step.

BlogsCampbell, CA
On this page
  1. 01Questions on this topic
  2. 02Ready to plan your project?

Planning · 7 min read · REF Builders

Most new homes, additions and ADUs need plans before construction can begin. Many homeowners are surprised to learn they do not need to have those plans before calling a builder. Bringing your general contractor in early can make the design process smoother and the construction more predictable.

Who does what

Each member of the team has a distinct role. Knowing who is responsible for what helps you ask the right questions at the right time.

Architect or designer

Develops the layout, look and drawings for the project.

Structural engineer

Designs the structural elements such as foundations, framing and connections.

General contractor

Builds the project to the approved plans, coordinates the trades and manages the schedule.

Why involve the builder during design

A builder can offer practical input while the plans are being prepared. Sometimes an engineering detail can be built more simply without changing what the design achieves, and catching that early saves time later.

At REF Builders we stay involved during design and engineering, then sign the construction agreement once the plans are approved.

From approved plans to construction

Once plans are approved, construction can be scheduled phase by phase. We explain what happens in each phase, how long it should take and which selections are due.

Choosing the right team

Look for a builder who communicates honestly, explains the process before it starts and documents changes in writing. Ask to see current work, photos and videos so you can judge quality for yourself.

Planning a project? We give every client a list of selection deadlines tied to each phase.
