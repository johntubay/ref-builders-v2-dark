Service overview

Full home remodeling, done properly

A full remodel touches nearly every trade in the house. Walls open, systems are reworked, and dozens of finish decisions need to be ready at the right moment. When those pieces are managed by different people, small gaps turn into delays and surprises.

REF Builders handles the whole sequence as your general contractor. Roberto leads the rough construction: foundations, framing and coordination with your engineer. Paula takes over as insulation and drywall begin, coordinating the finishes so the home comes together the way you planned it.

Scope

What this service covers

The exact scope follows your approved plans and agreement. These are the parts we typically coordinate.

01

Layout changes

Opening or reworking rooms as shown on your approved plans, with framing coordinated to the engineering.

02

Rough systems

Plumbing, electrical and mechanical rough work sequenced so inspections and selections line up.

03

Insulation and drywall

Coordinated as the handoff point between rough construction and finishes.

04

Kitchens and bathrooms

Often the center of a full remodel, planned with the layout and fixture selections needed during rough plumbing.

05

Flooring, trim and paint

Finish trades scheduled in order so each surface is protected and completed cleanly.

06

Turnkey completion

Final details, punch items and handover of a finished, move-in ready home.
