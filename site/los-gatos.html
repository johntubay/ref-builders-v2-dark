Building in Los Gatos

Los Gatos is close to home for us. Our office is in neighboring Campbell, and we have worked on projects in the Los Gatos area where clients wanted a builder who was easy to reach and closely involved.

Los Gatos homes range from established neighborhoods to hillside properties near the Santa Cruz Mountains. Whatever the setting, we work from approved plans and coordinate every phase through to the finishes.
