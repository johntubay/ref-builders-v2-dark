Privacy policy

RObedgardflam Corporation DBA REF Builders
Effective Date: November 25, 2026

RObedgardflam Corporation DBA REF Builders (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage your personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or use our services.

By using our website or submitting your information, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Personal information we collect

We may collect the following types of personal data from users:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address or project location
  • Service inquiry details or project information
  • IP address
  • Device and browser information
  • Website usage data (pages visited, time spent, interactions)
  • SMS communication preferences and consent status

2. How we use collected data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries and provide quotes or estimates
  • To schedule appointments and communicate about projects
  • To deliver requested services
  • To improve our website and customer experience
  • To send service updates, reminders, and transactional messages
  • To send marketing or promotional communications (only with consent)
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Sms consent: how it is obtained and used

By providing your phone number and opting in through our website forms, contact forms, or other communication channels, you consent to receive SMS messages from RObedgardflam Corporation DBA REF Builders.

SMS consent is obtained when:

  • You check a consent box on a web form
  • You submit your phone number for service inquiries
  • You verbally agree to receive SMS communications
  • You opt in through a text message confirmation process

SMS messages may include:

  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Project updates
  • Customer service communications
  • Promotional or marketing messages (if opted in)

Message frequency: daily. You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. Message and data rates may apply.

4. Cookies and tracking technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience and analyze website performance. These may include:

  • Session cookies
  • Analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics)
  • Advertising or remarketing pixels
  • Website performance and traffic monitoring tools

These technologies help us understand how users interact with our website and improve our services. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

5. How we protect your data

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Secure servers and hosting environments
  • Encryption where appropriate
  • Limited access to personal data
  • Internal policies to protect against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse

While no system is 100% secure, we take appropriate steps to safeguard your information.

6. Accessing or deleting your data

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal data

To make a request, please contact us using the information below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe in accordance with applicable laws.

7. Phone number sharing or selling

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your phone number or personal information with any third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Your phone number is used solely for communication between you and RObedgardflam Corporation DBA REF Builders. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties under any circumstances.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
