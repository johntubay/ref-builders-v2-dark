Where we build

REF Builders serves Santa Clara County, San Mateo County, Alameda County and San Francisco.

Our office is in Campbell, and we travel to projects across the South Bay, the Peninsula, the East Bay and San Francisco. We do not have offices in each city we serve. If your property is near one of these areas, contact us to confirm.
